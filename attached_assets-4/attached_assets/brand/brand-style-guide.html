Usage rules
✓ Do
Use Prussian Blue for primary actions and headings. Dusk Blue for body text. Off-White as page background.
✗ Don't
Don't use off-palette colors for primary UI. Legacy gold (#C49A2C) and blue (#1056F3) tokens are retired — remove them from code.
✓ Do
Use rgba overlays of brand colors for dark mode depth layers (#020F21, #021630). These are derived from Prussian Blue.
✗ Don't
Don't place Alabaster Grey text on white backgrounds — insufficient contrast. Use Dusk Blue (#3B506D) instead.
